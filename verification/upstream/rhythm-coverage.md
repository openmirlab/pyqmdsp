# Rhythm Upstream Parity Coverage

Fixture: `verification/upstream/fixtures/rhythm.json`, generated by
`build/original-rhythm/oracle_rhythm` against pristine `extern/qm-dsp`.

Covered direct upstream calls:

- `DetectionFunction`: all five `DF_*` modes, two time-domain frames per mode, two
  frequency-domain frames per mode with adaptive whitening enabled, a second
  no-whitening frequency-domain configuration with three sequential rising frames per
  mode, nonzero `DF_BROADBAND` fixture evidence, and `getSpectrumMagnitude()`
  after processing.
- `PeakPicking`: non-default threshold/window/delta parameters, mutated processed curve,
  and onset indices.
- `TempoTrack`: legacy tracker `process()` with `tempoReturn`.
- `TempoTrackV2`: constrained `calculateBeatPeriod()` plus non-default
  `calculateBeats()`.
- `DownBeat`: `setBeatsPerBar()`, `findDownBeats()`, `getBeatSD()`,
  `pushAudioBlock()`, `getBufferedAudio()`, and `resetAudioBuffer()`.
- `BeatSpectrum`: matrix self-similarity output.
- `beats()` convenience path: reproduced lower-level detection-function frames,
  trimmed detection function, `TempoTrackV2` periods/tempi/beats, seconds, BPM,
  and tempo curve; the public `rhythm.beats()` result is asserted against the same
  lower-level pipeline.

No rhythm/onset public numerical method is intentionally excluded. Constructors,
destructors, and raw construction structs are covered through the public Python
constructor parameters and object lifetime.
